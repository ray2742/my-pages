🧊 Recovery

Sauna + cold plunge post-workout all three days. Thursday after heavy legs is the most important recovery session of the week.

📈 Progression

Add weight or a rep every 1–2 weeks on big lifts (trap bar DL, bench, hack squat). Plyos stay at the same height/distance — focus on explosiveness and form, not load.
