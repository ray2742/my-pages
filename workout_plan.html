🧊 Recovery

Sauna + cold plunge post-workout all three days. Hot summer days: cut sauna in half or go cold-only. Thursday after heavy legs is the most important recovery session of the week.

🏒 Game-Day Mobility

Couch stretch 45 sec/side · 90/90 hold 45 sec/side · Deep squat hold 60 sec. Do these on game days before hitting the ice.

📈 Progression

Add weight or a rep every 1–2 weeks on big lifts (trap bar DL, bench, hack squat). Plyos and sprints stay at same height/distance — focus on explosiveness and form, not load.
