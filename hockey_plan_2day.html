Two lifts,
three games

Thursday keeps the strength. Saturday sharpens speed. Everything else is hockey and recovery.

Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat

Black days are game nights. Tap Thu or Sat to jump to that workout.

Thursday

Strength, about 45 min

Heavy weight, low volume, nothing to failure.

Warm-up, 5 min

  • Foam roll quads and glutes
  • Couch stretch, 30 sec/side
  • Adductor rock-backs, 8/side
  • Glute bridges, 10
  1. 1
    Sled push
    Heavy, about 50–75% of body weight on the sled
    4 × 15 yd
    Walk back
  2. 2
    Trap bar deadlift
    Stop with 1–2 reps left in the tank
    3 × 4–5
    2 min rest
  3. 3
    Bulgarian split squat
    2 × 6–8/side
    90 sec rest
  4. 4
    Barbell bench press
    3 × 5
    90 sec rest
  5. 5
    Lat pulldown
    3 × 8
    90 sec rest
  6. 6
    Adductor machine
    Groin injury insurance
    2 × 12
    60 sec rest
  7. 7
    Pallof press
    Or farmer’s walk, 2 × 40 yd
    2 × 10/side
    45 sec rest

Wrecked from the back-to-back? Drop every lift to 2 sets and keep the weight the same. The load maintains strength; the sets are expendable.

Saturday

Speed and game primer, about 35 min

Day before a game: prime the legs, don’t tire them.

Warm-up, 5 min

  • 90/90 hip switches, 8/side
  • Cossack squats, 6/side
  • Lateral band walks, 10/side
  • Leg swings, 10
  1. 1
    Box jumps
    Full reset between reps
    3 × 3
    60–90 sec rest
  2. 2
    Lateral skater jumps
    Continuous and fast, minimal ground contact
    3 × 4/side
    60–90 sec rest
  3. 3
    Out-and-back sprint
    Lean, sprint 10 yd, plant and turn, sprint back. The sled works as your marker.
    4 reps
    90+ sec rest
  4. 4
    Cable woodchop
    Arms long. Rotate from hips and upper back, not low back.
    3 × 8/side
    60 sec rest
  5. 5
    Face pulls
    3 × 15
    60 sec rest
  6. 6
    Wrist roller
    Then hammer pronation/supination, 2 × 10/side
    2–3 trips

Leave feeling faster than you showed up. If you’re gassed, burning, or sore, you overdid it.

Rest of the week

Monday
Most important recovery day. Pool walk or easy swim for 15 min, then sauna and cold plunge.
Thursday
Sauna and cold plunge are fine after lifting. Cold blunts strength gains, but you’re maintaining, so recovery wins.
Friday
Rest or a walk. Walks matter more now that the scale mostly depends on the kitchen.
Saturday
Keep the sauna short and hydrate. You play tomorrow.
Hot days
Cut sauna time in half or go cold only.
Game days
Couch stretch 45 sec/side, 90/90 hold 45 sec/side, deep squat hold 60 sec before the ice.
Progression
Keep loads heavy. Add weight only when the top of the rep range feels easy. Speed work gets sharper, not longer.
